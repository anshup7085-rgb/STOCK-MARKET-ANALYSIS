# Can this reach the goal? An audit against outside evidence (10 Nov 2026)

Asked by the holder: check the project, the market and what is available outside, and say honestly
whether intraday F&O can reach the goal, and what else could. **Checked** = verified today (in this
repository or in the sources at the end); **from memory** = not re-checked; **judgement** = my view.

The goal as the holder states it: ₹20,000 of capital, a steady ₹2,000–5,000 a day.

## 1. The daily target, in any market: not reachable. As sure as arithmetic

- ₹2,000 a day on ₹20,000 is **10% a day**, about 210% a month (checked: 2,000 x 21 / 20,000).
- Anyone who could make 10% a day steadily would simply leave the money in. ₹20,000 growing 10% a day
  for one trading year becomes about ₹4.5 x 10^14 (checked: 20,000 x 1.10^250), more than everything
  India produces in a year (from memory: roughly ₹350 lakh crore). Nobody has that, so nobody does it.
- What winners really make: in SEBI's study of FY26, the roughly 12% of individual derivatives
  traders who made money gained **₹1.22 lakh on average over the whole year**, about ₹490 a trading
  day, mostly on accounts far larger than ₹20,000 (checked, source 1).
- This is not about futures or options. No share, fund, commodity or currency changes the sum. The
  number that can change is the capital: ₹2,000 a day is 2.8% a month on ₹15 lakh, or 1.4% a month
  on ₹30 lakh (checked).

## 2. Intraday F&O on ₹20,000, for even a small steady income: very unlikely, not impossible

Evidence against (all checked unless marked):

- **SEBI's own counts of individual F&O traders.** FY22–FY24: 93% lost, ₹2 lakh each on average; 7.2%
  made any profit over the three years; about 1% made more than ₹1 lakh after costs (sources 2, 3).
  FY25: 91% lost (source 4). FY26: 87.7% lost; options were 92% of the losses; 59% of index-option
  turnover was on expiry day itself (sources 1, 5).
- **Who is on the other side.** In FY24 proprietary trading firms and foreign funds made gross profits
  of about ₹33,000 crore and ₹28,000 crore while individuals lost (source 2).
- **Our own tests, three years of minute data.** Nine strategies and seven explorations: none earns
  after costs. Buying an at-the-money option on a breakout, the nearest thing to what a small account
  does: 750 trades, mean result +0.002 of the risk, net −₹1,564 (`research/INTRADAY_LOG.md` step 12).
- **The one-lot problem.** One lot is the smallest trade. A stopped trade loses ₹1,300–4,000: 6–20% of
  the account. No method survives a normal losing run at that size (`STATUS.md`).
- **The one effect we did find favours sellers on expiry day.** Buyers of expiry-day options are the
  ones paying it, and expiry day is where most small buyers trade.

Why not 100%: four tests are built and unrun or waiting for data (first half hour against last half
hour; the opening gap; the expiry effect on four more indexes; the cousin's own rules once known).
They cost nothing more and finish by about 4 Dec. **Judgement:** about 19 chances in 20 that they
confirm the picture above. I will not call it 100% before they are run.

## 3. A cost the project had not counted

Kite Connect is ₹500 a month (checked, `research/OPPORTUNITY_MAP.md`): ₹6,000 a year, **30% of the
₹20,000**, before a single trade. Dhan was ₹499 + GST for one month. The server is free or about ₹700
a month depending on the AWS plan (unchecked: the holder's bill says which). So the tools alone can
cost a third to two-thirds of the capital each year. Paper research does not need Kite every month:
the history already downloaded and NSE's free files are enough for most of it.

## 4. What else there is for ₹20,000

| Path | What the evidence says | Fits ₹20,000? |
|---|---|---|
| NIFTY index fund, monthly SIP | About 10–12% a year over 20 years by most counts, with bad years as deep as −55% and long flat stretches (source 7). On ₹20,000 that is about ₹2,000 a year: small, but positive odds and no skill needed | Yes |
| Intraday shares (cash market) | SEBI, FY23: 71% of individuals lost; 80% of the frequent ones (source 6). But a trade can be as small as one share, so risk can be ₹100–200 a trade, which fixes the one-lot problem. Our paper engine, journal and risk checks already exist for shares | Yes, to research on paper |
| Swing trades in shares (days to weeks) | Our score and strategies A and B failed their tests in October. Costs weigh less than intraday | Yes, to research on paper |
| Intraday options and futures | Section 2 | Only bought options, at unsafe size |
| The holder's own earning power | A 100% return on ₹20,000 is ₹20,000. Saving ₹2,000 a month is ₹24,000 a year, with certainty. For a student, added savings and the skills this project teaches (Python, data, testing) are worth more than any return on this capital (judgement) | Yes |

## 5. The road that can reach ₹2,000–3,000 a day (judgement)

It is a capital goal reached over years, with a tested edge, not a strategy found this month:

1. Keep the machine honest and cheap: it has already stopped the holder from trading nine losing ideas.
2. Finish the four F&O tests by 4 Dec. Then decide once, on the evidence.
3. If none passes: pause intraday F&O, stop the monthly fees that are not needed, and turn the research
   to what ₹20,000 can size safely (shares, on paper, on data already paid for or free).
4. Meanwhile the ₹20,000 goes where the odds are positive (an index fund or a liquid fund) and grows
   by monthly savings. No rupee goes into F&O before a test passes.
5. Real trading starts when two things are true together: a strategy has passed its test, and the
   account is large enough to risk about 1% a trade.

## 6. Outside news the project must take in (reported; not yet checked against SEBI's circulars)

- From 7 Dec 2026, expiring contracts are to settle on the volume-weighted price of the last 30
  minutes, with a change to the last trading minutes on expiry day (source 5). It touches strategy V's
  settlement and every expiry-day idea; to be checked against the circular before 7 Dec.
- Lot sizes and expiry weekdays differ between secondary sources; the exchange notices decide.
