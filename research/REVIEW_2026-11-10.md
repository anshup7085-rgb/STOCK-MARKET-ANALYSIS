# Whole-project review (10 Nov 2026)

A fresh read of every document, the research logs and the F&O code, asked for by the holder: what to
update, delete and add to reach the goal (a steady income from intraday NSE F&O; paper first, then
small real money, every live order confirmed by the holder). **Checked** means verified in this
repository today; **from memory** means not re-checked.

## Where the project really stands

- **The machinery is sound.** All 18 test files pass (checked). Journals are hash-chained and verify.
  Rules are written before the data they judge. Costs are in every number.
- **No strategy earns yet.** Equity score: failed. Strategies A, B, T: failed. V-H (weekly iron condor,
  2019–2026): failed. Seven intraday explorations on Oct 2021–Oct 2024: every explorer strategy loses or
  is flat after costs; the opening-range breakout fails on futures and on options; the volatility
  premium adds nothing outside expiry day. One small effect stands: selling the at-the-money straddle on
  expiry day (about ₹17,000 a year a lot, on more than ₹1 lakh of margin).
- **So the gap is not code. It is where we look next, and what exactly we are aiming at.**

## Update

1. **Documents that now say untrue things** (checked): `README.md` describes an equities scanner and a
   status dated 17 Oct; `ROADMAP.md` still has "the current blocker: no market feed is reachable" and
   "Horizons: unblock a market feed"; `TRADING.md` "Known gaps" says Gate 1 has not been run (it ran and
   failed); `research/OPPORTUNITY_MAP.md` shows the events calendar as "NOW" since 28 Oct and the
   intraday replay as "NEXT" (it is done). Fix: one short status page that is the single truth, and
   correct these lines.
2. **The margin limit checks the wrong number.** The explorer limits margin to 80% of capital using its
   own estimate; on 6 Nov Zerodha's own figure was ₹4.71 lakh, 94% of the ₹5 lakh. Each entry already
   records Zerodha's figure; the limit should use it when it is there.
3. **The paper explorer's strategy list.** Three years of replay say its futures strategies lose about
   0.11 to 0.14 of the risk a trade, and the condor and fly lose too. Keeping them makes each day's report
   red for a known reason and raises the bar for everything else (the screen's bar is 20 times the number
   of strategies: 160 for eight). A new, shorter list: the expiry-day straddle variants, plus one or two
   kept only to measure real fills. Also: the straddle's ₹9,000 budget skips it when option prices are
   high (6 Nov: one unit risked ₹10,531), and those were its best expiry days in the replay.
4. **The exam is harder than the data can support.** A test at α = 0.005 on about 480 held-back sessions
   passes only a strategy that wins about 0.15 of its risk a trade, every day: a yearly Sharpe ratio above
   2, rare even among professionals. A real but ordinary edge would fail. Two honest ways out, neither a
   loosening by stealth: (a) more held-back trades (see Add 2); (b) the holder decides, in writing and
   before any result, whether a second, lower rung exists: a one-lot, defined-risk, hard-capped
   real-money pilot for a strategy that is positive in exploration and in the held-back years but short
   of the full bar. (b) is the holder's decision alone.

## Add

1. **A goal sheet, one page, numbers only:** real capital available, income wanted a month, the largest
   loss the holder accepts. Paper capital is ₹5,00,000. If real capital is much smaller, selling options
   or trading futures is not possible at all (one lot needs roughly ₹1.5–2.5 lakh of margin), and only
   bought options and small spreads fit. This decides which research is worth doing.
2. **More data while Dhan is paid for (to 4 Dec):** its documents say the same expired-options API
   serves other NSE and BSE index options (`exchangeSegment` NSE_FNO or BSE_FNO, any underlying; read
   today, not yet tried): BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY, MIDCPNIFTY, SENSEX. In 2021–2024 these had weekly
   expiries on other weekdays, so the one effect we found can be checked on four to five times as many
   expiry days; SENSEX still has a weekly expiry, which doubles the held-back expiry days. Check the
   server's free disk first.
3. **Replication of the expiry-day effect on those indexes**, rules fixed before their data is read. An
   effect that repeats on an index it was not found on is worth far more than a better-looking backtest.
4. **The events calendar** (RBI policy, Budget, election results, US Fed), from official sources:
   promised on 28 Oct, not built. Every strategy needs to know those days.
5. **A stopping rule for the whole search,** agreed now: a date or a number of further idea families
   after which, if nothing has passed, intraday F&O is set aside. SEBI's own studies found about nine in
   ten individual F&O traders lose money (from memory). The system's value so far is the money it has
   stopped the holder from losing; a stopping rule protects that.
6. **A daily check of the simulator:** replay each day from the minutes we save and compare with what
   the paper book did. It measures how far the three-year replays can be trusted.
7. **The live F&O path, only once something passes:** today `trading/fno/` can price and margin an order
   but cannot place one (checked). Live needs: order placement for several legs, stop orders resting at
   the exchange (the paper stop is looked at once a minute), the broker's margin checked before each
   order, phone-tap confirmation, a static IP. Roughly two to three weeks of work; paper to real money is
   not a switch.
