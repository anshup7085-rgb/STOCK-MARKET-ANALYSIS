# The closing-auction rules and the 7 Dec 2026 change: what was checked (27 Nov 2026)

The project's records said, from secondary sources: "from 7 Dec 2026 expiring contracts settle on the
volume-weighted price of the last 30 minutes" (research/AUDIT_2026-11-10.md, section 6; to be checked against
the circular before 7 Dec). This is that check. Each line says how it is known.

## Verified: NSE's own circulars, read as PDFs from nsearchives.nseindia.com on 27 Nov 2026

- **NSE/CMTR/73362, 18 Mar 2026** (after SEBI circular SEBI/HO/47/11/11(3)2025-MRD-POD2/I/2765/2026 of 16 Jan
  2026): a Closing Auction Session (CAS) in the equity cash segment, first for the stocks that have
  derivatives; a stock's CAS reference price is the VWAP of its trades from 15:00 to 15:15.
- **NSE/FAOP/75472, 30 Jul 2026**: the changes in the equity derivatives segment that come with CAS are live
  from **3 Aug 2026** (it refers to NSE/FAOP/74467 of 29 May 2026 for what they are).
- **NSE/FAOP/76186, 4 Sep 2026**: a changed pre-open call auction in equity derivatives, live from
  **7 Sep 2026** (details in NSE/FAOP/74970 of 1 Jul 2026, not read).
- NSE's 2026 holiday circulars (also read): see `data/nse_holidays.json`.

## Reported, not read in the original

- **NSE/FAOP/74467, 29 May 2026** (a broker's copy, through search): the F&O normal market close moved from
  15:30 to **15:40** from 3 Aug 2026.
- **SEBI's review of 15 Oct 2026 and NSE's two circulars of 6 Nov 2026** (several press reports agree; SEBI's
  site refuses this research workspace's connections and NSE's November circular numbers were not found):
  from **7 Dec 2026**
  - the cash-market closing auction runs **15:30 to 15:40** (closing at a random moment from 15:38);
  - equity derivatives trade until **15:45**;
  - **expiring index derivatives settle on the index value computed from its constituents' VWAP over the
    last 30 minutes of the cash session, 15:00 to 15:30**: the closing auction no longer sets the settlement;
  - one report: the daily (non-expiry) closing price of derivatives stays a half-hour VWAP, moved to 15:15 to
    15:45; some order-handling provisions start on 8 Feb 2027.
- **Before 3 Aug 2026** (from memory, not checked here): a stock's closing price was the VWAP of its last 30
  minutes, and the index's close, which expiring index options settled on, was built from those prices.

So the reported claim in our records is right in substance, and the fuller picture is three settlement regimes:

| Period | How expiring NIFTY options settled | Status of this line |
|---|---|---|
| to 2 Aug 2026 | the index close, built from the last 30 minutes' prices | from memory |
| 3 Aug to 4 Dec 2026 | with the closing auction in the cash market | reported |
| from 7 Dec 2026 | the index from its constituents' 15:00-15:30 VWAP | reported (SEBI 15 Oct, NSE 6 Nov) |

## What it means for this project

1. **Settlement accounting needs no change.** Strategy V and anything held to expiry read NSE's own final
   settlement price (`SttlmPric`) from its daily F&O file, whatever method produced it
   (research/PREREGISTRATION_3.md assumed neither method, by design).
2. **The exploration years (to Oct 2024) and the forward record from 7 Dec share a settlement regime;
   3 Aug to 4 Dec 2026 is different.** Any test or record that touches those weeks (the held-back years end
   in Oct 2026; the expiry-day straddle's forward record began in November) must mark them.
3. **Trading hours.** F&O has closed at 15:40 since 3 Aug (reported) and will close at 15:45 from 7 Dec
   (reported). The explorer stops at 15:30 and squares off at 15:15: earlier than the market, the safe side;
   no change. Option and index minutes are read from 09:15 to 15:29: right for the index (the cash session
   still ends at 15:30); for options it leaves out their last minutes, which no strategy here uses.
4. **Found and fixed: the option-minute saver** (`tools/save_expiring_options.py`) asked Kite for candles only
   up to 15:30 and started at 15:40. So every file it saved since it began (30 Oct 2026) lacks each day's
   last 10 minutes of option trading, and from 7 Dec it would have started while the market was open. It
   now starts at 15:50 and asks for the whole day. Files already saved stay as they are.
5. **Before any live use:** `run_trader.py`'s watch loop takes its after-close pass at 15:40, which assumes a
   15:30 close. It must follow the real close (15:45) before it is ever run live. Live is not running.
