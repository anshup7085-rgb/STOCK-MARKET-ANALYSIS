# Gate 1 on real NSE data — 2026-10-17

The first run of the scoring model against real market history. ROADMAP set
the pass criteria before any result existed; this report applies them as
written.

**Verdict: Gate 1 NOT PASSED. The score does not predict outcomes.** By the
ROADMAP's own kill criterion — "no relationship between score and outcome" — the
current scoring model is retired as a ranking tool. Nothing in this report
justifies paper or live trading it.

## What was run

```bash
python run_backtest.py --provider yfinance --lookback 2500 --horizon 20 --step 5 \
    --window 300 --json scans/gate1_yf_10y.json
python run_trader.py gates --backtest scans/gate1_yf_10y.json
```

- **Data:** yfinance, EOD, delayed — split-adjusted daily OHLCV, last bar 2026-10-16.
- **Universe:** the 47-name default list; 46 returned data (`TATAMOTORS.NS` no
  longer resolves on Yahoo — see limitations).
- **Period:** signals from 2017-06-28 to 2026-09-16 (2,500 bars per name, the
  first 200 used as warm-up).
- **Method:** unchanged from `market/backtest.py` — causal snapshot at every 5th
  bar, entry at the signal close, stop/target settled on real highs and lows,
  stop taken when a bar spans both, gaps fill at the open, 20-bar time exit. One
  change made before the run, for fidelity: each snapshot now sees the trailing
  300 bars, which is what the live scanner fetches, instead of all history to
  date (otherwise ten-year-old swing highs become "targets" the live system
  never sees). Net R deducts the delivery fee card (unverified defaults) and
  0.05% slippage per leg.
- **No parameter was changed after seeing results.**

## What this does and does not mean

- It means **this score does not rank NSE large caps usefully** over a 20-session
  horizon. Not weakly; not at all.
- It does **not** mean the execution layer is wrong. Brokers, risk controls,
  journal, gates and alerts measure and act on whatever strategy they are
  given; they just did their first job, which was to say no.
- It does **not** license searching the same data for a band, horizon or filter
  that would have worked. Every such search spends the data. A score ≥ 60 slice
  does look marginally better (+0.034R net, n = 3,110, 95% CI [−0.013, +0.081]),
  and picking it now, after seeing it, is exactly the curve fit the ROADMAP's
  third kill criterion names.

## Data limitations

- **Survivorship bias, in the strategy's favour.** The universe is today's large
  caps. Names that fell out of the index are missing, so real results would
  likely be worse, not better.
- **`TATAMOTORS.NS` returned no data.** The ticker appears to have changed after a
  corporate restructuring; the default universe in `market/universe.py` predates
  it. Not replaced here — the replacement symbol should be confirmed on NSE.
- **Yahoo data is split-adjusted and delayed**, with occasional bad ticks. Dividends
  are not in the price series (auto_adjust is off), which slightly understates
  long returns.
- **Charges are unverified defaults.**

## Next

Retire this score as a ranking signal. Any new strategy starts with a written,
committed pre-registration — the hypothesis, the exact rules, the data split
and the pass criteria — **before** its test data is looked at, and is judged once
on data it was not designed on. See ROADMAP.
