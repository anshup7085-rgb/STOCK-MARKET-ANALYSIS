# Round 2 on real data — strategy T — 2026-10-22

**Verdict: FAIL — NO TRADE. T is not evaluable on Yahoo's history.** The
pre-registered data rules stopped the test at the audit, before any signal,
weight or return of T was computed. Under the registration that is a fail, the
data may not be repaired after it has been seen, and T and its variants may not
be re-tested on this history (`research/PREREGISTRATION_2.md`, sections 3, 12
and 21).

This is a finding about the **data**, not about trend timing. It says nothing
either way about whether T would have worked. It does mean the one free source
this environment can reach is not good enough for a pre-registered test of
NSE ETFs before 2014.

## What was registered

Strategy T: hold each of NIFTYBEES and GOLDBEES, in two half-capital sleeves,
only while its month-end close is above its 10-month average (Faber 2007),
else cash. It was to be judged against an exposure-matched twin by a
Newey–West t ≥ 2.576, with eight guards and an after-tax deployment condition.
Data: Yahoo, EOD, delayed; NIFTYBEES.NS, GOLDBEES.NS and ^NSEI only.

## Why it stops

**1. Sixteen month-ends with no usable trading day (sections 3.2 and 4).** A row
counts only if it traded: close above zero and volume above zero. Yahoo's
history reports zero volume on most days of 2012–13 for both ETFs:

| Zero-volume rows | 2009 | 2010 | 2012 | 2013 | 2015 | 2024–26 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| NIFTYBEES | 6 | 3 | 42 | 119 | 1 | 4 |
| GOLDBEES | 6 | 12 | 210 | 119 | — | 3 |

So for every month-end from 2012-02-29 to 2013-05-31 (holding months t = 26–41)
there is no session within the next five on which both ETFs have a genuine row,
and X_t does not exist. These may be real days of no trade, or volumes Yahoo
never recorded; the rule does not distinguish, by design.

**2. Prices wrong by 10× and 100× around the December 2019 unit splits (sections
3.3–3.5).**

| Close | 2019-12-18 | 2019-12-19 | 2019-12-20 | 2019-12-23 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| NIFTYBEES | 129.25 | **13.02** | **13.03** | 129.93 |
| GOLDBEES | 33.60 | **0.34** | **0.34** | 33.65 |

Each drop matches a split factor (1/10, 1/100), but the price returns two
sessions later, so the persistence test fails and the event is not confirmed.
The unadjusted ratios (0.10 and 0.01) then breach the block rule (below 0.80),
and the text is explicit: "There is no manual fix once the data has been seen."

## What this does and does not mean

- **No pass/fail call about trend timing exists.** T was not wrong or right; it
  was never run. Under the registration it is still a FAIL, and slot 5 of the
  lifetime budget is spent: five remain.
- **T and its variants are dead on this history** (section 21), even on cleaner
  data. Re-testing T on another source would be a second look at the same
  history, which is what the budget exists to prevent.
- **The free data is the binding constraint.** Yahoo's NSE ETF history has
  missing volumes for 2012–13 and broken split days in 2019. Any future
  historical test of ETFs needs a source with verified corporate actions and
  real volumes (the exchange's own bhavcopy files, or Kite's historical API),
  checked before registration.

## Where this leaves the project

Three rounds, five hypotheses, no measured edge. The correct output is still
**NO TRADE**. What remains honest to do is set out in the ROADMAP: register any
new idea **forward-only**, on data dated after its own commit, and paper-trade
it from there; or bring in a verified data source for new ideas on the five
remaining slots.
